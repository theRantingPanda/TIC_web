As an expatriate in Singapore, you have several options for health insurance. Understanding the differences between international and local plans helps you make the right choice for your situation.

## Local Singapore Plans (MediShield Life / Integrated Shield Plans)

MediShield Life is Singapore’s national basic health insurance scheme for Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents. Integrated Shield Plans (IPs), offered by selected local insurers, build on MediShield Life to cover private hospital treatment. However, these plans are generally not available to foreigners on work passes and do not provide global coverage.

## International Health Insurance

International health insurance is designed for expatriates and globally mobile individuals. It provides continuous coverage worldwide (or in defined regions), regardless of where you are located. These plans can cover you in Singapore, back home, and while travelling.
